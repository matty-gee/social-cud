## social distance specifically can be improved as a measure

The online sample shows an effect of distance and social avoidance - but the in-person samples dont
- not in affil or power, or in their ratio either

**Explanation**
Small amounts of difference in distance can only meaningfully when the objects are close enough; when they are all very far away, distance saturates and stops meaningfully separating people
Distance is joint (both dimensions), reference-frame specific deviation from an anchor point
- If many participants are close to the anchor, small behavioral differences translate into meaningful differences in distance.
- If nearly everyone is far away, distances are all large and similar (“saturation”): individual differences in distance are compressed and become less informative.
The online sample acts more affiliatively on average, i.e. closer to max affiliation anchor; so this gives distance senstivity to individual difference 

**How to test this, and find a better measure?** 
z-score affiliation and power within each sample to remove sample mean shifts 
define anchor in z-units and compute distance in the standardized space
